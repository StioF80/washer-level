Washer levelling

Let’s get your washer level and steady

A level washer with all four feet taking weight runs quieter, walks less during spin and lasts longer. Your phone does the measuring.

Place your phone flat in the middle of the lid, with the top of the phone pointing to the back of the machine. Remove any case with a thick back.

Calibrate your phone

Place your phone flat in the middle of the lid, top pointing to the back, then tap Measure.

Measuring twice, turned around, cancels out any tilt from your phone’s camera bump or case.
Motion sensors aren’t available here, so this is a simulation. Drag the bubble to tilt the washer.

Check all four feet are taking weight

Leave your phone where it is. When prompted, press down firmly on each corner of the lid, away from the phone.

Nearly done: lock it in

Your washer is level and steady. Lock the feet so it stays that way.

  • 1Tighten the lock nut on each foot up against the base of the machine.
  • 2Run a short rinse and spin with the drum empty and listen for knocking or walking.
  • 3If it moves during spin, run the check again. Floors can settle.
